07 / OPTIONS & RECOMMENDATION

A practical direction.
A controlled decision.

OPTION A / PROVISIONAL RECOMMENDATION

Managed repository.
Accountable workflow.

Central supplier records with versioned evidence, review tasks and renewal tracking.

Fit: a bounded document and review process.
Condition: confirm capabilities, licensing and support ownership.
OPTION B / ALTERNATIVE

Custom application

Greater design flexibility, with development and ongoing support responsibilities.

PROPOSED EVALUATION WEIGHTS
Requirements fit 30%
Security & auditability 25%
Delivery feasibility 20%
Usability 15%
Ongoing ownership 10%
